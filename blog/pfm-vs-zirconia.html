PFM vs Zirconia Crowns: A Practical Guide for Dental Clinics

The best material depends on the clinical indication, aesthetic requirement, preparation, occlusion, patient factors and laboratory workflow.

PFM

Porcelain-fused-to-metal restorations remain a familiar option for many practices. They can be a practical choice when established workflows, strength requirements and economics are important.

Zirconia

Zirconia offers a metal-free restorative option with different grades and workflows. The laboratory prescription, design, shade communication and finishing protocol all influence the final result.

What clinics should communicate

  • Tooth number and restoration type
  • Preparation and margin information
  • Shade and aesthetic expectations
  • Occlusion and contact considerations
  • Digital scan or impression details
  • Any special clinical instructions

The DenZest approach

DenZest focuses on the coordination around the restoration: clear communication, selected partner laboratories, pickup and delivery, packaging and follow-up. The goal is to reduce operational friction without interfering with the clinician's decision-making.

This article is general workflow information, not a substitute for clinical judgment or a laboratory prescription.
